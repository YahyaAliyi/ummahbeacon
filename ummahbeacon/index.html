About the founder

A Servant Leader Dedicated to the Ummah

For 16 years, I served the Ethiopian people in public leadership — from district governor responsible for 130,000 citizens, to Jimma Zone level serving nearly 4 million people.

Those years taught me the weight of Amanah (trust), the discipline of systems, and the power of servant leadership. My journey began in 2005 as Amir of Muslim Students at Dilla University.

Today, I dedicate my life to helping the Ummah thrive — with integrity, barakah, and excellence — through Islamic knowledge, coaching, and practical resources that combine faith with real-world impact.

Our foundation

Five Core Islamic Principles

Every piece of teaching, every resource, and every coaching session is grounded in these eternal values.

نِيَّة
Niyyah · Intention
Every action begins with pure intention. Success is measured by the sincerity of your purpose.
أَمَانَة
Amanah · Trust
You are accountable for every trust placed in you — from your family to your community.
صِدْق
Sidq · Truthfulness
Honesty in all dealings, promises, and relationships. Shortcuts compromise integrity.
إِحْسَان
Ihsan · Excellence
Pursue excellence in every detail — your worship, your work, and your service to others.
مَسْؤُولِيَّة
Mas'uliyyah · Accountability
You are responsible for your impact on the Ummah. Choose every action wisely.
