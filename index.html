Our Principles

  • Niyyah (Intention): Begin with pure purpose — every step guided by sincerity.
  • Amanah (Trust): Honor commitments — trust is the foundation of community.
  • Sidq (Truthfulness): Speak with clarity — truth lights the path of integrity.
  • Ihsan (Excellence): Strive for beauty — excellence transforms effort into worship.
  • Accountability: Stand transparent — responsibility strengthens faith and unity.
