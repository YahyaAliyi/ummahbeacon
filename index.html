🌙 Our Mission

Ummah Beacon exists to uplift the Muslim Ummah through ethical business, education, and spiritual guidance. We shine a light of sincerity, prosperity, and unity for generations to come.

✨ Our Projects

  • Halal Global Marketing: Ethical business education for a just economy.

  • Spiritual Assets: Visual reminders and prayers to inspire daily life.

🌟 A Servant Leader Dedicated to the Ummah

For 16 years, I served the Ethiopian people in public leadership — from district governor responsible for 130,000 citizens, to Jimma Zone level serving nearly 4 million people.

Those years taught me the weight of Amanah (trust), the discipline of systems, and the power of servant leadership. My journey began in 2005 as Amir of Muslim Students at Dilla University.

Today, I dedicate my life to helping the Ummah thrive — with integrity, barakah, and excellence — through Islamic knowledge, coaching, and practical resources that combine faith with real‑world impact.
